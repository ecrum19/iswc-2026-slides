# Presentation assets

Keep images, diagrams, and other presentation-specific files in this directory.
Reference them from index.html with paths such as assets/hero.jpg.

Prefer descriptive, lowercase filenames. Optimize large images before committing
them so the generated deck stays quick to load and easy to archive.

The title slide uses the logos in assets/logos/ for FWO, Ghent University, VITO,
and IDLab. Replace those files only when the affiliations represented by the
deck change.

The screenshots in assets/reports/ show the headlines of the news articles, bug
reports, incident reports and service announcements cited on slide 13 (masthead,
headline and, where shown, the date), captured on 27 October 2026. Where a
headline alone does not say what happened, a highlighted line from the page
follows a "[…]" gap. Each one links to its source on the slide.
